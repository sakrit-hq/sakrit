# evidence/ — the Act I dossier

Primary evidence that the problem is real, specific, and expensive — the thing
the whole plan is built on, not vibes.

Collect here:

- **War stories** — concrete, dollars-and-hours accounts from people who hit
  this in production. The gate is at least five specific ones.
- **Hand-rolled partial fixes found in the wild** — teams who built our
  competitor, badly. The gate is at least two.
- **Interview notes** — from practitioners running agents that touch real
  systems (aim for ten teams).
- **Framework confirmations** — the vendor docs and open GitHub issues where
  the frameworks admit the problem themselves (already catalogued in the
  roadmap's Grounding section).

**Stop-if signal to watch for:** teams telling us "we just don't retry" — and
meaning it.
